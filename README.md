# Customer-Churn-Analysis-for-Telecomms
This project focuses on analysing customer churn in the telecommunications industry to understand customer behaviour, identify factors contributing to customer attrition, and provide actionable recommendations for improving customer retention.
The dataset contains customer demographics, account information, subscribed services, contract details, payment methods, monthly charges, total charges, and churn status.

Dataset Description
The dataset includes the following key information:
•	Customer Demographics: Gender, senior citizen status, partner, and dependents.
•	Account Information: Customer tenure, monthly charges, and total charges.
•	Services Subscribed: Phone service, internet service, technical support, online security, backup, and entertainment services.
•	Contract & Payment Details: Contract type, paperless billing, and payment method.
•	Customer Churn: Indicates whether a customer has discontinued the company's services.

Workload Performed

1.	Data Inspection: Examined the dataset to understand its structure, columns, data types, and overall quality.
2.	Data Cleaning: Checked for missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent entries, and formatting issues to improve data accuracy.
3.	Exploratory Data Analysis: Analysed customer demographics, service subscriptions, contract types, payment methods, and billing patterns.
4.	Churn Analysis: Compared customers who discontinued their services with those who remained, identifying customer groups with higher churn rates.
5.	Revenue Analysis: Examined monthly and total charges to understand the financial implications of customer attrition.
6.	Customer Retention Recommendations: Developed data-driven recommendations aimed at reducing customer churn, improving customer satisfaction, and encouraging long-term subscriptions.
   



Tools Used
•	Microsoft Excel
•	Excel Formulas
•	Pivot Tables
•	Data Cleaning and Visualisation

Project Objective
To transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights that can help telecommunications companies understand customer attrition, minimise revenue loss, and develop more effective customer retention strategies.
